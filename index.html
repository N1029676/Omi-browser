Reading does not erase anything on the Omi. Downloads are saved in this browser, so you can reload the page or resume after a dropped connection.
Free space on the Omi…

Once everything is downloaded, you can tell the Omi it may reuse the space taken by that audio. This removes the audio from the device: the Omi phone app can no longer sync it and the firmware will overwrite it. Your copy in this browser then becomes the only one. Available only after a complete download that was saved to browser storage.
